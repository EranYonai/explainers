An AI can draw anything in seconds. So why is drawing not where an explainer starts?

You have seen explainers: a picture you step through, with a caption under it. It is natural to assume someone began with a sketch and animated it. Follow one small topic, Why does a cache forget things?, through the real order of work. You give the answers; the plan below changes to match.

Drawing comes late because by then everything it shows is already decided.

The interview fixes who it is for, the beats fix the story, the states fix each frame, the code fixes each number, and the check catches what drifted. Start with the canvas and you decide all of that by accident.
